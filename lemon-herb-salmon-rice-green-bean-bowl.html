Lemon-Herb Salmon, Rice & Green Bean Bowl

A satisfying, high-protein salmon lunch with fluffy brown basmati rice, tender green beans and a creamy lemon-herb Greek yogurt sauce.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Measurement rules: Salmon is weighed raw before cooking. Brown basmati rice is weighed after cooking. Green beans, garlic and dill are weighed raw. Greek yogurt and Dijon mustard are weighed as packaged. Olive oil is measured separately.

Ingredients

  • 150 g raw boneless Atlantic salmon
  • 110 g cooked Wegmans Basmati Brown Rice
  • 140 g raw green beans
  • 100 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 3 g extra-virgin olive oil
  • 20 g fresh lemon juice
  • 8 g Dijon mustard
  • 5 g fresh dill, raw
  • 5 g garlic, raw
  • 2 g fresh lemon zest
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 1 g dried oregano

Directions

  1. Cook brown basmati rice separately and weigh 110 g after cooking.
  2. Preheat oven to 400°F. Place raw salmon on a lined baking sheet.
  3. Rub salmon with olive oil, minced garlic, lemon zest, oregano and black pepper.
  4. Bake for approximately 12–16 minutes, until the internal temperature reaches 145°F.
  5. Steam green beans until tender-crisp.
  6. Mix Greek yogurt, lemon juice, Dijon mustard and chopped fresh dill into a creamy sauce.
  7. Place cooked rice in a bowl. Add green beans and baked salmon.
  8. Spoon lemon-herb sauce over the top and serve warm.
