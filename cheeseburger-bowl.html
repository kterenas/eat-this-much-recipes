Cheeseburger Bowl

All the flavors of a cheeseburger in a high-protein bowl with lean beef, potatoes, cheddar, pickles and creamy burger sauce.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Ground beef is weighed raw. Potatoes are weighed after cooking. No additional cooking oil is used.

Ingredients

  • 180 g raw 96% lean ground beef
  • 140 g cooked baby potatoes
  • 100 g lettuce
  • 100 g tomato
  • 40 g onion
  • 50 g dill pickles
  • 20 g reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 40 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 10 g yellow mustard
  • 10 g ketchup
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Weigh 180 g raw 96% lean ground beef. Season with garlic powder and black pepper and cook thoroughly without added oil.
  2. Cook the baby potatoes and weigh 140 g after cooking.
  3. Mix the Greek yogurt, mustard and ketchup to make the burger sauce.
  4. Add the lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles and potatoes to a bowl.
  5. Add the cooked ground beef and sprinkle the cheddar over it.
  6. Finish with the burger sauce and serve.
