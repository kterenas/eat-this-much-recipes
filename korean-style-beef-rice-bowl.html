Korean-Style Beef Rice Bowl

A high-protein Korean-style beef and brown rice bowl with edamame, cucumber, carrots, ginger and sesame.

Servings: 1

ETM Nutrition: 495 calories | 50 g protein | 37 g carbohydrates | 17 g fat

Measurement rule: Ground beef is measured raw because that is the ETM food entry used for this recipe. Rice and edamame are measured after cooking.

Ingredients

  • 190 g raw 96% lean ground beef
  • 110 g cooked brown basmati rice
  • 50 g cooked shelled edamame
  • 75 g cucumber
  • 50 g carrots
  • 10 g reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 5 g sesame oil
  • 5 g rice vinegar
  • 5 g fresh scallions
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 5 g fresh ginger
  • 1 g crushed red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Cook the 96% lean ground beef in a nonstick skillet until completely cooked.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger, reduced-sodium soy sauce and crushed red pepper to the beef and stir to combine.
  3. Place 110 g cooked brown basmati rice in a bowl.
  4. Add the cooked beef, cooked edamame, cucumber and carrots.
  5. Drizzle with rice vinegar and exactly 5 g sesame oil.
  6. Finish with fresh scallions and serve.
