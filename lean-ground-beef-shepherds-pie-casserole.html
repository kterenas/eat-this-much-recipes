Lean Ground Beef Shepherd's Pie Casserole

A hearty high-protein family casserole with savory lean ground beef, peas, carrots and a creamy mashed potato topping.

Servings: 4

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 35 minutes

Measurement rules: Ground beef, potatoes, carrots, onion and garlic are weighed raw. Potatoes are weighed after peeling but before cooking. Peas are weighed thawed. Broth, yogurt, tomato paste and cheese are weighed as packaged. All quantities are for the entire four-serving casserole, not one serving.

Ingredients

  • 720 g raw 96% lean ground beef
  • 600 g raw peeled Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 160 g frozen green peas, thawed
  • 160 g raw carrots
  • 120 g raw yellow onion
  • 12 g raw garlic
  • 200 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 60 g reduced-fat sharp cheddar cheese
  • 240 g reduced-sodium beef broth
  • 40 g tomato paste
  • 20 g Worcestershire sauce
  • 8 g olive oil
  • 12 g cornstarch
  • 3 g dried thyme
  • 2 g dried rosemary
  • 2 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F. Peel and dice potatoes, then boil for 15–20 minutes until fork-tender. Drain thoroughly.
  2. Mash potatoes with 200 g nonfat Greek yogurt and 30 g reduced-fat cheddar cheese.
  3. Heat 8 g olive oil in a large skillet. Add diced onion, carrots and minced garlic. Cook for approximately 5 minutes.
  4. Add ground beef and brown thoroughly until it reaches 160°F.
  5. Stir in tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, thyme, rosemary and black pepper.
  6. Mix cornstarch with a small amount of cold beef broth. Add this mixture and the remaining broth to the skillet. Simmer until thickened.
  7. Fold in thawed peas and transfer the beef mixture to a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.
  8. Spread mashed potatoes evenly over the beef. Sprinkle with remaining 30 g cheddar.
  9. Bake uncovered for 15–20 minutes until heated through and lightly golden.
  10. Rest for 5 minutes. Weigh the finished casserole and divide into four equal portions.
