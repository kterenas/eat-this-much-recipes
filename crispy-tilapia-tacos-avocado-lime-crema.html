Crispy Tilapia Tacos with Avocado-Lime Crema

Crispy seasoned tilapia in high-fiber tortillas, topped with crunchy cabbage slaw and a creamy avocado-lime Greek yogurt sauce.

Servings: 1

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 15 minutes

Measurement rules: Tilapia, avocado, cabbage, red onion and cilantro are weighed raw. Panko is weighed dry. Greek yogurt is weighed as packaged. Tortillas are weighed as packaged. All quantities are for one serving.

Ingredients

  • 200 g raw boneless tilapia fillet
  • 90 g Ole High Fiber tortilla wraps (2 wraps)
  • 15 g dry panko breadcrumbs
  • 35 g raw avocado flesh
  • 70 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 60 g raw red cabbage
  • 20 g raw red onion
  • 20 g fresh lime juice
  • 5 g fresh cilantro, raw
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 2 g smoked paprika
  • 2 g chili powder
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g ground cumin
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven or air fryer to 400°F. Cut raw tilapia into strips.
  2. Season tilapia with smoked paprika, chili powder, garlic powder, cumin and black pepper.
  3. Press 15 g dry panko breadcrumbs onto the fish and drizzle with 5 g olive oil.
  4. Air-fry for approximately 8–12 minutes or bake for 12–15 minutes, until golden and the fish reaches an internal temperature of 145°F.
  5. Mash 35 g avocado with 70 g nonfat Greek yogurt and 10 g fresh lime juice.
  6. Toss shredded red cabbage and sliced red onion with the remaining 10 g lime juice and cilantro.
  7. Warm the tortillas. Divide the crispy tilapia, cabbage slaw and avocado-lime crema evenly between the two wraps. Serve immediately.
