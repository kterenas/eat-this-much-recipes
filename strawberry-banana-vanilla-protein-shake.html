Strawberry Banana Vanilla Protein Shake

A creamy, fruit-forward, high-protein shake made with Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla whey protein isolate, strawberries, banana, oats, almond butter and Greek yogurt.

Servings: 1

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 0 minutes

Measurement rules: Weigh the protein powder, dry oats, almond butter, frozen strawberries, peeled banana, Greek yogurt and almond milk in grams. The banana weight excludes the peel. The oats are weighed dry, before blending. Ice is optional and does not materially affect calories or macronutrients. All quantities are for one complete shake.

Ingredients

  • 30.5 g Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla whey protein powder
  • 150 g unsweetened frozen strawberries
  • 100 g peeled banana
  • 30 g dry rolled oats
  • 24 g unsweetened almond butter
  • 100 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 240 g unsweetened almond milk
  • 100 g ice, optional

Directions

  1. Add 240 g unsweetened almond milk and 100 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt to a blender.
  2. Add 30.5 g Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla whey protein powder, 30 g dry rolled oats and 24 g unsweetened almond butter.
  3. Add 100 g peeled banana and 150 g unsweetened frozen strawberries.
  4. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth.
  5. Add up to 100 g ice if desired and blend again. Serve immediately as one complete shake.
