BBQ Chicken, Corn & Potato Bowl

Smoky BBQ chicken with tender potatoes, sweet corn, crunchy cabbage and a creamy Dijon yogurt dressing.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken is weighed raw. Potatoes and corn are weighed after cooking. No additional cooking oil is used.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 150 g cooked baby potatoes
  • 75 g cooked corn
  • 100 g shredded cabbage or coleslaw mix
  • 30 g BBQ sauce
  • 50 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 10 g Dijon mustard
  • 10 g apple cider vinegar
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Weigh 170 g raw chicken breast. Season with garlic powder, smoked paprika and black pepper and cook without added oil.
  2. Cook the baby potatoes and weigh 150 g cooked.
  3. Cook the corn and weigh 75 g cooked.
  4. Mix the Greek yogurt, Dijon mustard and apple cider vinegar.
  5. Add the potatoes, corn and cabbage to a bowl.
  6. Slice the chicken and place it over the vegetables.
  7. Add the BBQ sauce and drizzle with the yogurt-Dijon dressing.
