Lemon-Garlic Parmesan Tilapia & Roasted Potatoes

A high-protein dinner featuring lemon-garlic tilapia with a Parmesan topping, golden roasted baby potatoes, tender asparagus and a creamy lemon-parsley yogurt sauce.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 35 minutes

Measurement rules: Tilapia, baby potatoes, asparagus, garlic and parsley are weighed raw. Parmesan and Greek yogurt are weighed as packaged. Olive oil is measured separately. All quantities are for one serving.

Ingredients

  • 200 g raw boneless tilapia fillet
  • 150 g raw baby potatoes
  • 120 g raw asparagus
  • 20 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 12 g extra-virgin olive oil
  • 50 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 15 g fresh lemon juice
  • 5 g raw garlic
  • 5 g fresh parsley, raw
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Wash and cut baby potatoes into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Toss potatoes with 8 g olive oil and roast on a lined baking sheet for approximately 20 minutes.
  3. Trim asparagus and toss with the remaining 4 g olive oil.
  4. Season raw tilapia with minced garlic, smoked paprika, black pepper and 10 g lemon juice. Sprinkle with 20 g grated Parmesan.
  5. After the potatoes have roasted for 20 minutes, add the asparagus and tilapia to the oven.
  6. Bake for approximately 10–15 minutes until the fish reaches 145°F. Continue roasting potatoes until tender and golden.
  7. Combine Greek yogurt with the remaining 5 g lemon juice and chopped fresh parsley.
  8. Serve tilapia with roasted potatoes, asparagus and lemon-parsley yogurt sauce.
