Lemon Chicken, Orzo & Spinach Soup

A bright, comforting chicken soup featuring tender chicken breast, orzo pasta, fresh spinach, carrots, lemon and dill.

Servings: 1

Measurement rules: Chicken breast is weighed raw. Orzo pasta is weighed dry. Vegetables are weighed raw. All liquids are measured in grams.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 40 g dry orzo pasta
  • 75 g raw baby spinach
  • 60 g raw carrots
  • 40 g raw onion
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 350 g low-sodium chicken broth
  • 12 g olive oil
  • 20 g fresh lemon juice
  • 5 g fresh dill
  • 1 g dried oregano
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook chicken breast to 165°F, then shred or dice. The chicken measurement is its raw weight.
  2. Heat olive oil in a soup pot. Add carrots and onion. Cook for 4–5 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and oregano. Cook for 30 seconds.
  4. Add chicken broth and bring to a gentle boil.
  5. Add dry orzo pasta and cook according to package directions until tender.
  6. Add cooked chicken and spinach. Simmer for 2–3 minutes.
  7. Remove from heat. Stir in fresh lemon juice, chopped dill and black pepper.
  8. Serve warm. Add water if needed for a thinner broth.
