Garlic Parmesan Chicken & Potato Bowl

Tender chicken breast, baby potatoes and broccoli with a creamy garlic Parmesan sauce.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken is weighed raw. Potatoes and broccoli are weighed cooked.

Ingredients

  • 160 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 140 g cooked baby potatoes
  • 150 g cooked broccoli
  • 15 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 75 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 7 g olive oil
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 5 g lemon juice
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g Italian seasoning
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the baby potatoes and weigh 140 g cooked.
  2. Season the raw chicken with garlic powder, Italian seasoning and black pepper and cook thoroughly. Slice into bite-size pieces.
  3. Cook the broccoli until tender-crisp.
  4. Mix the Greek yogurt, fresh garlic, lemon juice and Parmesan together for the sauce.
  5. Combine the chicken, potatoes and broccoli in a bowl.
  6. Drizzle with the measured olive oil and top with the garlic Parmesan sauce.
