Apple-Cinnamon Protein Baked Oatmeal

Warm baked oatmeal with fresh apple, cinnamon, cottage cheese, egg whites and peanut butter. A high-protein breakfast that can be made ahead.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Measurement rules: Oats are weighed dry. Egg whites and apple are weighed raw. Cottage cheese, peanut butter and almond milk are weighed as packaged.

Ingredients

  • 35 g dry old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 150 g Friendship 1% low-fat cottage cheese
  • 200 g raw liquid egg whites
  • 70 g raw apple, diced and cored
  • 20 g natural peanut butter
  • 60 g unsweetened almond milk
  • 2 g ground cinnamon
  • 3 g baking powder
  • 3 g vanilla extract
  • Zero-calorie sweetener to taste (optional)

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly spray a small baking dish.
  2. Blend cottage cheese, egg whites, almond milk, peanut butter and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Combine dry oats, cinnamon and baking powder.
  4. Stir the blended mixture into the oats. Fold in diced apple and optional sweetener.
  5. Pour into the prepared baking dish.
  6. Bake for 25–30 minutes until the center is set and reaches 160°F.
  7. Cool for 5 minutes before serving.
