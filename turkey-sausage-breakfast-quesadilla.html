Turkey Sausage Breakfast Quesadilla

A crispy high-protein breakfast quesadilla filled with turkey sausage, eggs, vegetables and melted cheddar, served with avocado and salsa.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Turkey sausage is weighed according to its ready-to-cook package entry. Egg whites and whole egg are weighed before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 1 Ole High Fiber tortilla wrap
  • 60 g turkey breakfast sausage
  • 150 g liquid egg whites
  • 50 g whole egg
  • 20 g reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 40 g bell pepper
  • 30 g onion
  • 30 g avocado
  • 50 g salsa
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • Cooking spray

Directions

  1. Cook the turkey breakfast sausage thoroughly and set aside.
  2. Coat a skillet with cooking spray and sauté the pepper and onion.
  3. Add the egg whites and whole egg and cook until set.
  4. Season with garlic powder and smoked paprika.
  5. Place the tortilla in a clean nonstick skillet.
  6. Add the egg mixture, turkey sausage and cheddar to one half.
  7. Fold the tortilla over and cook until lightly browned.
  8. Flip and brown the other side.
  9. Serve with 30 g avocado and 50 g salsa.
