Creamy Chicken, Broccoli & Cheddar Soup

A rich and creamy high-protein soup with tender chicken, broccoli, sharp cheddar, potatoes and Greek yogurt.

Servings: 1

Measurement rules: Chicken is weighed raw. Potatoes are weighed cooked. Broccoli, carrots, onion and garlic are weighed raw.

Ingredients

  • 150 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 150 g raw broccoli florets
  • 110 g cooked baby potatoes
  • 25 g sharp cheddar cheese
  • 80 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 40 g raw carrots
  • 40 g raw onion
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 250 g low-sodium chicken broth
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 5 g Dijon mustard
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook baby potatoes separately. Weigh 110 g cooked and dice into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Cook chicken breast to 165°F. Shred or dice.
  3. Heat olive oil in a soup pot. Add onion and carrots. Cook for 4–5 minutes.
  4. Add garlic and smoked paprika. Cook 30 seconds.
  5. Add chicken broth and broccoli. Simmer 8–10 minutes.
  6. Blend about one-third of the broccoli and broth, then return to the pot.
  7. Add cooked chicken, potatoes, Dijon mustard and black pepper.
  8. Reduce heat to low and stir in cheddar until melted.
  9. Remove from heat. Temper Greek yogurt with warm broth and stir into the soup. Do not boil.
  10. Serve warm.
