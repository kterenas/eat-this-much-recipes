Smoky Turkey, Sweet Potato & Black Bean Chili

A hearty high-protein chili with lean ground turkey, tender sweet potatoes, black beans, tomatoes and smoky spices.

Servings: 1

Measurement rules: Ground turkey is weighed raw. Sweet potato is weighed after cooking. Black beans are weighed after draining. Vegetables are weighed raw.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw 93% lean ground turkey
  • 100 g cooked sweet potato
  • 80 g canned black beans, drained
  • 120 g canned diced tomatoes
  • 60 g raw red bell pepper
  • 50 g raw onion
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 200 g low-sodium chicken broth
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 15 g tomato paste
  • 3 g chili powder
  • 2 g smoked paprika
  • 2 g ground cumin
  • 1 g dried oregano
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 5 g fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. Cook sweet potato until tender. Weigh 100 g cooked and dice into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Heat olive oil in a soup pot. Add ground turkey and brown, breaking it into small pieces.
  3. Add onion and red bell pepper. Cook for 4–5 minutes.
  4. Add garlic, tomato paste, chili powder, smoked paprika, cumin, oregano and black pepper. Cook for 1 minute.
  5. Add diced tomatoes, chicken broth and drained black beans.
  6. Simmer gently for 12–15 minutes until turkey reaches 165°F.
  7. Stir in cooked sweet potato and simmer for 2–3 minutes.
  8. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve.
