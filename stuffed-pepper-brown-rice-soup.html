Stuffed Pepper & Brown Rice Soup

A hearty, high-protein soup featuring lean ground beef, colorful bell peppers, brown basmati rice and a savory tomato broth.

Servings: 1

Measurement rules: Ground beef is weighed raw. Brown basmati rice is weighed after cooking. Vegetables are weighed raw.

Ingredients

  • 180 g raw 95% lean ground beef
  • 90 g cooked Wegmans Basmati Brown Rice
  • 75 g raw red bell pepper
  • 75 g raw green bell pepper
  • 150 g canned diced tomatoes
  • 50 g raw onion
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 300 g low-sodium beef broth
  • 8 g olive oil
  • 15 g tomato paste
  • 5 g Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 g Italian seasoning
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 5 g fresh parsley

Directions

  1. Cook brown basmati rice separately. Weigh 90 g cooked.
  2. Heat olive oil in a soup pot. Brown ground beef, breaking it into small pieces.
  3. Add red and green bell peppers and onion. Cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Add garlic, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, smoked paprika and black pepper. Cook 1 minute.
  5. Add diced tomatoes, beef broth and Worcestershire sauce.
  6. Simmer gently for 15 minutes until vegetables are tender and beef is fully cooked.
  7. Stir in cooked brown rice and heat for 2–3 minutes.
  8. Garnish with parsley and serve.
