Mediterranean Tuna, Chickpea & Artichoke Salad

A fresh Mediterranean-inspired salad featuring protein-rich tuna, chickpeas, artichoke hearts, crisp vegetables, feta cheese and lemon dressing.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: None

Measurement rules: Tuna, chickpeas and artichoke hearts are weighed after draining. Chickpeas are also rinsed. Cucumber, tomatoes, onion and parsley are weighed raw. Feta is weighed as packaged. Use water-packed artichokes, not oil-marinated.

Ingredients

  • 150 g canned tuna in water, drained
  • 110 g canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 80 g water-packed artichoke hearts, drained
  • 100 g raw cucumber
  • 100 g raw cherry tomatoes
  • 35 g reduced-fat feta cheese
  • 12 g extra-virgin olive oil
  • 20 g fresh lemon juice
  • 30 g raw red onion
  • 10 g fresh parsley
  • 1 g dried oregano
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Drain tuna and weigh 150 g. Drain and rinse chickpeas, then weigh 110 g. Drain artichoke hearts and weigh 80 g.
  2. Dice cucumber, halve cherry tomatoes, chop red onion and quarter artichoke hearts.
  3. Combine tuna, chickpeas, artichokes, cucumber, tomatoes and red onion.
  4. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, oregano and black pepper.
  5. Pour dressing over salad and toss gently.
  6. Top with crumbled feta and chopped parsley. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
