Tropical Mango Pineapple Protein Shake

A creamy tropical protein shake made with Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla whey protein, sweet mango, pineapple, Greek yogurt, coconut milk beverage, almond butter and oats.

Servings: 1

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 0 minutes

Measurement rules: Weigh protein powder, frozen mango, frozen pineapple, Greek yogurt, coconut milk beverage, almond butter, dry oats and shredded coconut in grams. Fruit is weighed frozen, and oats are weighed dry before blending. Use unsweetened coconut milk beverage from a carton, not canned coconut milk. Ice is optional and does not materially affect calories or macros. All quantities are for one complete shake.

Ingredients

  • 40 g Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla whey protein powder
  • 100 g unsweetened frozen mango chunks
  • 80 g unsweetened frozen pineapple chunks
  • 150 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 240 g unsweetened coconut milk beverage from a carton
  • 25 g natural unsweetened almond butter
  • 15 g dry rolled oats
  • 8 g unsweetened shredded coconut
  • 100 g ice, optional

Directions

  1. Add 240 g unsweetened coconut milk beverage and 150 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt to a blender.
  2. Add 40 g Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla whey protein powder, 100 g frozen mango and 80 g frozen pineapple.
  3. Add 25 g natural almond butter, 15 g dry rolled oats and 8 g unsweetened shredded coconut.
  4. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth and creamy.
  5. Add up to 100 g ice if desired and blend again. Serve immediately as one complete shake.
