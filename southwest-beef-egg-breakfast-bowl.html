Southwest Beef & Egg Breakfast Bowl

A hearty, high-protein breakfast bowl featuring seasoned lean ground beef, fluffy egg whites, brown basmati rice, fresh avocado and salsa.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Measurement rules: Ground beef and liquid egg whites are weighed raw. Brown basmati rice is weighed after cooking. Vegetables, avocado and cilantro are weighed raw. Salsa is weighed as packaged. No olive oil is included; use nonstick cooking spray.

Ingredients

  • 130 g raw 95% lean ground beef
  • 150 g raw liquid egg whites
  • 100 g cooked Wegmans Basmati Brown Rice
  • 40 g raw avocado flesh
  • 60 g raw red bell pepper
  • 40 g raw onion
  • 50 g salsa
  • Nonstick cooking spray, as needed
  • 2 g ground cumin
  • 2 g chili powder
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 5 g fresh cilantro
  • 10 g fresh lime juice

Directions

  1. Cook brown basmati rice separately. Weigh 100 g after cooking.
  2. Spray a nonstick skillet with cooking spray. Add raw ground beef and cook over medium heat, breaking it into crumbles.
  3. Add cumin, chili powder, garlic powder and black pepper. Cook beef until it reaches 160°F.
  4. Add diced bell pepper and onion. Cook for 3–5 minutes until softened.
  5. In a separate nonstick skillet, scramble 150 g liquid egg whites until fully set.
  6. Place cooked brown basmati rice in a bowl. Add seasoned beef, vegetables and egg whites.
  7. Top with salsa, diced avocado, cilantro and fresh lime juice. Serve warm.
