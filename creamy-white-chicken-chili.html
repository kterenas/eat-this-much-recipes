Creamy White Chicken Chili

A hearty high-protein white chicken chili with tender chicken, cannellini beans, green chiles, cheddar and a creamy Greek yogurt finish.

Servings: 1

Measurement rules: Chicken is weighed raw. Cannellini beans and green chiles are weighed after draining. Vegetables are weighed raw.

Ingredients

  • 150 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 120 g canned cannellini beans, drained
  • 80 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 20 g reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 60 g canned diced green chiles, drained
  • 50 g raw onion
  • 50 g raw green bell pepper
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 250 g low-sodium chicken broth
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 2 g ground cumin
  • 2 g chili powder
  • 1 g dried oregano
  • 10 g fresh lime juice
  • 5 g fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. Cook chicken breast to 165°F, then shred. The chicken measurement is its raw weight.
  2. Heat olive oil in a soup pot. Add onion and green bell pepper. Cook for 4–5 minutes.
  3. Add garlic, cumin, chili powder and oregano. Cook for 30 seconds.
  4. Add chicken broth, drained cannellini beans, green chiles and shredded chicken.
  5. Simmer gently for 10–15 minutes.
  6. Mash about one-third of the beans to thicken the chili naturally.
  7. Reduce heat to low and stir in cheddar until melted.
  8. Remove from heat. Temper Greek yogurt with warm broth and stir into the chili. Do not boil.
  9. Add lime juice, garnish with cilantro and serve.
