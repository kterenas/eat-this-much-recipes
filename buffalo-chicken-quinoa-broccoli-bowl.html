Buffalo Chicken, Quinoa & Broccoli Bowl

A satisfying, high-protein lunch featuring spicy Buffalo chicken, fluffy white quinoa, roasted broccoli, cheddar and a creamy homemade cottage cheese ranch dressing.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Measurement rules: Chicken breast and quinoa are weighed after cooking. Broccoli is weighed raw before roasting. Cottage cheese, Buffalo sauce and cheddar are weighed as packaged. Olive oil is measured separately.

Ingredients

  • 130 g cooked skinless chicken breast
  • 120 g cooked Wegmans White Quinoa
  • 130 g raw broccoli florets
  • 75 g Friendship 1% low-fat cottage cheese
  • 25 g Buffalo wing sauce
  • 8 g olive oil
  • 15 g reduced-fat shredded cheddar cheese
  • 10 g fresh lemon juice
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g dried dill
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 10–20 g water, to thin dressing

Directions

  1. Cook white quinoa separately. Weigh 120 g after cooking.
  2. Cook chicken breast to 165°F. Weigh 130 g after cooking and slice.
  3. Preheat oven to 425°F. Toss broccoli with 8 g olive oil and spread on a baking sheet.
  4. Roast broccoli for 15–20 minutes until tender and lightly browned.
  5. Blend cottage cheese, lemon juice, garlic powder, dill, black pepper and a little water until smooth.
  6. Toss cooked chicken with Buffalo wing sauce.
  7. Place quinoa in a bowl. Add roasted broccoli and Buffalo chicken.
  8. Sprinkle with cheddar and drizzle with cottage cheese ranch dressing.
