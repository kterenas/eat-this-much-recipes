Apple-Cinnamon Cottage Cheese Oat Bowl

Warm oatmeal with creamy cottage cheese, cinnamon apples, walnuts and a touch of maple syrup.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Oats are weighed dry before cooking. Cottage cheese, apple, walnuts and maple syrup are measured in grams.

Ingredients

  • 35 g dry old-fashioned oats
  • 200 g Friendship 1% Low Fat No Salt Added Cottage Cheese
  • 100 g apple, diced
  • 10 g walnuts, chopped
  • 5 g pure maple syrup
  • 2 g ground cinnamon
  • 2 g vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Cook the oats with water according to package directions.
  2. Cook the diced apple with the cinnamon in a nonstick skillet until softened.
  3. Stir the cottage cheese and vanilla into the warm oatmeal.
  4. Top with the cinnamon apples, walnuts and maple syrup.
