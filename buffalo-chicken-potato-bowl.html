Buffalo Chicken & Potato Bowl

A high-protein Buffalo chicken bowl with potatoes, green beans, creamy cottage cheese sauce and blue cheese.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken, potatoes and green beans are measured after cooking. No additional cooking oil is included.

Ingredients

  • 165 g cooked skinless chicken breast
  • 140 g cooked baby potatoes
  • 150 g cooked green beans
  • 25 g Buffalo sauce
  • 60 g low-fat cottage cheese
  • 15 g crumbled blue cheese
  • 10 g fresh scallions
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the chicken thoroughly and weigh 165 g after cooking.
  2. Cook the baby potatoes and weigh 140 g after cooking.
  3. Cook the green beans and weigh 150 g after cooking.
  4. Blend the cottage cheese with a small amount of the Buffalo sauce until smooth and creamy.
  5. Toss the cooked chicken with the remaining Buffalo sauce and garlic powder.
  6. Place the potatoes and green beans in a bowl and top with the Buffalo chicken.
  7. Drizzle with the cottage cheese sauce and finish with blue cheese, scallions and black pepper.
