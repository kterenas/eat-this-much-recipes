Turkey Taco Loaded Baked Potato

A satisfying high-protein lunch featuring a fluffy baked potato topped with taco-seasoned ground turkey, black beans, salsa, melted cheddar and a creamy cottage cheese topping.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 55 minutes

Measurement rules: Ground turkey and russet potato are weighed raw before cooking. Black beans are weighed after draining and rinsing. Vegetables and cilantro are weighed raw. Cottage cheese, cheddar and salsa are weighed as packaged. Use cooking spray instead of measured olive oil.

Ingredients

  • 150 g raw 93% lean ground turkey
  • 150 g raw russet potato
  • 60 g canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 60 g Friendship 1% low-fat cottage cheese
  • 20 g reduced-fat shredded cheddar cheese
  • 50 g salsa
  • 50 g raw red bell pepper
  • 30 g raw red onion
  • 0 g olive oil (nonstick cooking spray)
  • 2 g ground cumin
  • 2 g chili powder
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 5 g fresh cilantro, raw
  • 10 g fresh lime juice

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Scrub and pierce the raw russet potato. Bake for 45–60 minutes until tender, or microwave until fully cooked.
  2. Coat a nonstick skillet with cooking spray. Add raw ground turkey, cumin, chili powder, garlic powder and smoked paprika.
  3. Cook turkey over medium heat, breaking into crumbles, until it reaches 165°F.
  4. Add diced red bell pepper and red onion. Cook until softened.
  5. Stir in drained black beans and salsa. Heat thoroughly.
  6. Blend cottage cheese until smooth.
  7. Split the baked potato and fluff with a fork. Spoon the turkey and bean mixture over the potato.
  8. Top with shredded cheddar, creamy cottage cheese, fresh cilantro and lime juice. Serve warm.
