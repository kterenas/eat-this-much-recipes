Buffalo Chicken Banza Pasta Salad

A cold, high-protein pasta salad with Buffalo chicken, crunchy vegetables, creamy Greek-yogurt dressing and blue cheese.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken is weighed raw. Banza pasta is weighed dry before cooking. No additional cooking oil is used.

Ingredients

  • 130 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 42 g dry Banza chickpea pasta
  • 75 g celery
  • 75 g carrots
  • 75 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 25 g Buffalo sauce
  • 15 g crumbled blue cheese
  • 10 g fresh scallions
  • 5 g lemon juice
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the Banza according to package directions. Drain, rinse under cold water and allow to cool.
  2. Cook the chicken without added oil, cool and cut into bite-size pieces.
  3. Mix the Greek yogurt, Buffalo sauce, lemon juice, garlic powder and black pepper.
  4. Combine the Banza, chicken, celery, carrots and scallions.
  5. Add the Buffalo Greek-yogurt dressing and toss well.
  6. Top with crumbled blue cheese and chill before serving.
