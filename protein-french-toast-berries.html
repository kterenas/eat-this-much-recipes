Protein French Toast with Berries

High-protein French toast topped with creamy Greek yogurt, fresh berries and a touch of maple syrup.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Use 3 slices (81 g) Schmidt Old Tyme 647 Wheat Bread. Egg and egg whites are measured before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 3 slices (81 g) Schmidt Old Tyme 647 Wheat Bread
  • 150 g liquid egg whites
  • 50 g whole egg
  • 100 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 100 g strawberries
  • 50 g blueberries
  • 10 g pure maple syrup
  • 5 g butter
  • 2 g ground cinnamon
  • 2 g vanilla extract

Directions

  1. Whisk the egg whites, whole egg, cinnamon and vanilla together.
  2. Dip the bread into the egg mixture, coating both sides.
  3. Melt the butter in a nonstick skillet over medium heat.
  4. Cook the French toast until golden on both sides and cooked through.
  5. Top with Greek yogurt, strawberries, blueberries and maple syrup.
