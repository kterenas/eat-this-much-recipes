Chocolate Peanut Butter Protein Shake

A rich and creamy chocolate peanut butter meal-replacement shake made with Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla protein powder, cocoa, peanut butter, banana, Greek yogurt and oats.

Servings: 1

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 0 minutes

Measurement rules: Weigh protein powder, peanut butter, cocoa powder, peeled banana, Greek yogurt, almond milk and dry rolled oats in grams. Banana weight excludes the peel. Oats are weighed dry before blending. Ice is optional and does not materially affect calories or macronutrients. All quantities are for one complete shake.

Ingredients

  • 40 g Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla whey protein powder
  • 30 g natural creamy peanut butter, unsweetened
  • 12 g unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 100 g peeled banana
  • 150 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 240 g unsweetened almond milk
  • 20 g dry rolled oats
  • 100 g ice, optional

Directions

  1. Add 240 g unsweetened almond milk and 150 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt to a blender.
  2. Add 40 g Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla protein powder, 12 g unsweetened cocoa powder and 30 g natural creamy peanut butter.
  3. Add 100 g peeled banana and 20 g dry rolled oats.
  4. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth and creamy.
  5. Add up to 100 g ice if desired and blend again. Serve immediately as one complete shake.
