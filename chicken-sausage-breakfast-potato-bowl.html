Chicken Sausage Breakfast Potato Bowl

A hearty high-protein breakfast bowl with chicken sausage, crispy potatoes, eggs, peppers, onions and melted cheddar.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Potatoes are weighed after cooking. Use the chicken sausage nutrition entry that matches your actual product in Eat This Much.

Ingredients

  • 100 g chicken breakfast sausage
  • 125 g cooked baby potatoes
  • 125 g liquid egg whites
  • 50 g whole egg
  • 75 g bell pepper
  • 50 g onion
  • 20 g reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the chicken sausage and slice into bite-size pieces.
  2. Cook the baby potatoes and weigh 125 g cooked.
  3. Heat the olive oil and cook the potatoes, pepper and onion with the seasonings.
  4. Add the whole egg and egg whites and scramble.
  5. Add the cooked chicken sausage and combine.
  6. Transfer to a bowl and top with cheddar.
