Hearty Beef, Barley & Vegetable Soup

Lean beef, tender pearl barley, green beans, carrots and mushrooms in a savory beef broth.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Ground beef is weighed raw. Pearl barley is weighed after cooking. Vegetables are weighed raw.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw 95% lean ground beef
  • 90 g cooked pearl barley
  • 80 g raw green beans
  • 60 g raw carrots
  • 75 g raw mushrooms
  • 50 g raw onion
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 350 g low-sodium beef broth
  • 10 g olive oil
  • 15 g tomato paste
  • 5 g Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 g dried thyme
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 5 g fresh parsley

Directions

  1. Cook pearl barley separately. Weigh 90 g after cooking.
  2. Heat olive oil in a soup pot. Brown ground beef, breaking it into small pieces.
  3. Add onion, carrots and mushrooms. Cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Add garlic, tomato paste and thyme. Stir for 1 minute.
  5. Add beef broth, green beans, Worcestershire sauce and black pepper.
  6. Simmer for 15–20 minutes until vegetables are tender and beef is fully cooked.
  7. Stir in cooked pearl barley and simmer for 2–3 minutes.
  8. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve.
