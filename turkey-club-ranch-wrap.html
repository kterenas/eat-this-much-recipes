Turkey Club Ranch Wrap

A high-protein turkey club with turkey bacon, avocado, cheddar, fresh vegetables and creamy Greek-yogurt ranch.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Turkey breast and turkey bacon are weighed ready to eat. Use one Ole High Fiber tortilla wrap.

Ingredients

  • 1 Ole High Fiber tortilla wrap
  • 120 g sliced roasted turkey breast
  • 30 g cooked turkey bacon
  • 25 g reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 40 g avocado
  • 75 g tomato
  • 50 g lettuce
  • 60 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 3 g dry ranch seasoning
  • 5 g Dijon mustard

Directions

  1. Mix the Greek yogurt, ranch seasoning and Dijon mustard.
  2. Warm the Ole High Fiber tortilla until pliable.
  3. Layer the lettuce, tomato and turkey breast in the center.
  4. Add the turkey bacon, avocado and cheddar.
  5. Spoon the Greek-yogurt ranch dressing over the filling.
  6. Fold in the sides, roll tightly and serve.
