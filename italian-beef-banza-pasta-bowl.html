Italian Beef & Banza Pasta Bowl

A hearty Italian-style pasta bowl made with lean ground beef, Banza chickpea pasta, marinara, mushrooms, spinach and melted cheese.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Banza pasta, ground beef, mushrooms and spinach are weighed cooked.

Ingredients

  • 60 g cooked Banza chickpea pasta
  • 120 g cooked 95% lean ground beef
  • 100 g marinara sauce
  • 75 g cooked mushrooms
  • 50 g cooked spinach
  • 15 g part-skim mozzarella cheese
  • 5 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 g Italian seasoning
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g crushed red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Cook the Banza pasta and weigh 60 g cooked.
  2. Cook the 95% lean ground beef thoroughly, drain if necessary, and weigh 120 g cooked.
  3. Cook the mushrooms and spinach until tender.
  4. Add the marinara sauce, Italian seasoning, garlic powder and crushed red pepper to the beef and heat thoroughly.
  5. Add the Banza pasta, mushrooms and spinach to the beef and marinara mixture and toss together.
  6. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan and serve warm.
