Turkey Taco Stuffed Sweet Potato

A taco-seasoned sweet potato stuffed with lean turkey and black beans, then topped with salsa, avocado, Greek yogurt and cheddar.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Ground turkey is measured raw. Sweet potato is measured after cooking. Black beans are weighed after draining.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw 93% lean ground turkey
  • 150 g cooked sweet potato
  • 40 g canned black beans, drained
  • 50 g salsa
  • 50 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 30 g avocado
  • 20 g reduced-fat shredded cheddar cheese
  • 10 g lime juice
  • 2 g ground cumin
  • 2 g chili powder
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 5 g fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. Cook the sweet potato until tender and weigh 150 g after cooking.
  2. Cook 170 g raw 93% lean ground turkey in a nonstick skillet until completely cooked.
  3. Season the turkey with cumin, chili powder and smoked paprika.
  4. Warm the drained black beans and salsa.
  5. Split the cooked sweet potato and fill with the seasoned turkey and black beans.
  6. Top with salsa, Greek yogurt, avocado, reduced-fat cheddar, lime juice and cilantro.
