Mediterranean Tilapia, Chickpea & Artichoke Skillet

A Mediterranean-style tilapia dinner with tender chickpeas, artichoke hearts, tomatoes, spinach, crumbled feta and fresh lemon.

Servings: 1

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Measurement rules: Tilapia, cherry tomatoes, spinach, red onion, garlic and parsley are weighed raw. Chickpeas and artichokes are weighed after draining. Feta is weighed as packaged. All quantities are for one serving.

Ingredients

  • 200 g raw boneless tilapia fillet
  • 100 g canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 80 g water-packed artichoke hearts, drained
  • 100 g raw cherry tomatoes
  • 60 g raw baby spinach
  • 30 g reduced-fat feta cheese
  • 12 g extra-virgin olive oil
  • 30 g raw red onion
  • 20 g fresh lemon juice
  • 5 g raw garlic
  • 5 g fresh parsley
  • 1 g dried oregano
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Drain and rinse chickpeas. Drain artichoke hearts. Halve cherry tomatoes, dice red onion and mince garlic.
  2. Heat 7 g olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add red onion and garlic and cook until softened.
  3. Add chickpeas, artichoke hearts, cherry tomatoes, oregano and black pepper. Cook for 5–7 minutes.
  4. Add spinach and cook until wilted. Stir in 10 g fresh lemon juice.
  5. Pat dry the tilapia. Heat the remaining 5 g olive oil in a separate nonstick skillet. Cook fish approximately 3–5 minutes per side until its internal temperature reaches 145°F.
  6. Transfer chickpea and vegetable mixture to a plate. Place the cooked tilapia on top.
  7. Finish with 30 g crumbled reduced-fat feta, 5 g chopped parsley and the remaining 10 g fresh lemon juice.
