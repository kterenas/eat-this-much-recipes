Beef & Broccoli Brown Rice Bowl

Lean beef and broccoli with brown basmati rice and a savory ginger-garlic sauce.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Ground beef is weighed raw. Brown rice and broccoli are weighed cooked.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw 95% lean ground beef
  • 100 g cooked Wegmans Basmati Brown Rice
  • 175 g cooked broccoli
  • 10 g reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 5 g sesame oil
  • 5 g rice vinegar
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 5 g fresh ginger
  • 10 g scallions
  • 1 g crushed red pepper flakes

Directions

  1. Cook the brown basmati rice and weigh 100 g cooked.
  2. Cook the broccoli until tender-crisp and weigh 175 g cooked.
  3. Cook the ground beef thoroughly in a skillet.
  4. Add the garlic and ginger and cook until fragrant.
  5. Add the soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar and crushed red pepper and stir well.
  6. Add the broccoli and toss with the beef mixture.
  7. Serve over the brown rice and top with scallions.
