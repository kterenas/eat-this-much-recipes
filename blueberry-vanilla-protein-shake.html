Blueberry Vanilla Protein Shake

A creamy blueberry vanilla protein shake made with Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla whey protein, blueberries, Friendship low-fat cottage cheese, almond butter and oats.

Servings: 1

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 0 minutes

Measurement rules: Weigh protein powder, frozen blueberries, cottage cheese, almond butter, dry rolled oats, almond milk and cinnamon in grams. Blueberries are weighed frozen, and oats are weighed dry before blending. Ice is optional and does not materially affect calories or macronutrients. All quantities are for one complete shake.

Ingredients

  • 35 g Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla whey protein powder
  • 140 g unsweetened frozen blueberries
  • 150 g Friendship 2% low-fat cottage cheese
  • 25 g natural unsweetened almond butter
  • 20 g dry rolled oats
  • 240 g unsweetened almond milk
  • 1 g ground cinnamon
  • 100 g ice, optional

Directions

  1. Add 240 g unsweetened almond milk and 150 g Friendship 2% low-fat cottage cheese to a blender. Blend for 15–20 seconds until smooth.
  2. Add 35 g Dymatize ISO100 Gourmet Vanilla protein powder, 140 g frozen blueberries, 25 g natural almond butter, 20 g dry rolled oats and 1 g ground cinnamon.
  3. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth and creamy.
  4. Add up to 100 g ice if desired and blend again. Serve immediately as one complete shake.
