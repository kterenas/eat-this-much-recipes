Chicken Fried Brown Rice

A lighter, high-protein fried rice with chicken, brown basmati rice, egg, vegetables, ginger and sesame.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken is weighed raw. Brown rice and vegetables are weighed cooked.

Ingredients

  • 150 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 100 g cooked brown basmati rice
  • 75 g liquid egg whites
  • 50 g whole egg
  • 75 g cooked peas and carrots
  • 75 g cooked broccoli
  • 10 g reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 5 g sesame oil
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 5 g fresh ginger
  • 10 g fresh scallions

Directions

  1. Weigh 150 g raw chicken breast, cut into bite-size pieces and cook thoroughly.
  2. Add the broccoli and peas and carrots and cook until heated.
  3. Push everything to one side of the pan. Add the whole egg and egg whites and scramble.
  4. Add 100 g cooked brown basmati rice, garlic and ginger.
  5. Add the soy sauce and sesame oil and stir-fry until everything is hot and evenly combined.
  6. Finish with fresh scallions.
