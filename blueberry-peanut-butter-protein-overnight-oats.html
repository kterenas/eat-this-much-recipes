Blueberry Peanut Butter Protein Overnight Oats

Creamy, high-protein overnight oats with blueberries, peanut butter, cottage cheese, Greek yogurt and chia seeds. A convenient make-ahead breakfast.

Servings: 1

Preparation: 10 minutes

Refrigeration: 6–8 hours

Measurement rules: Oats and chia seeds are weighed dry. Blueberries are weighed raw. Cottage cheese, yogurt, peanut butter and almond milk are weighed as packaged.

Ingredients

  • 35 g dry old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 200 g Friendship 1% low-fat cottage cheese
  • 100 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 70 g raw blueberries
  • 25 g natural peanut butter
  • 60 g unsweetened almond milk
  • 5 g dry chia seeds
  • 1 g ground cinnamon
  • 3 g vanilla extract
  • Zero-calorie sweetener to taste (optional)

Directions

  1. Blend cottage cheese, Greek yogurt, almond milk, vanilla and optional sweetener until smooth.
  2. Combine dry oats, chia seeds and cinnamon in a bowl.
  3. Add the blended cottage cheese mixture and stir well.
  4. Gently fold in blueberries.
  5. Transfer to a jar or airtight container. Swirl peanut butter over the top.
  6. Cover and refrigerate for 6–8 hours or overnight. Serve chilled.
