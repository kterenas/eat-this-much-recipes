Teriyaki Salmon Rice Bowl

Teriyaki Atlantic salmon with brown basmati rice, broccoli, edamame, ginger and scallions.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Salmon is measured raw. Rice, broccoli and edamame are measured after cooking. No additional cooking oil is used.

Ingredients

  • 140 g raw Atlantic salmon
  • 90 g cooked brown basmati rice
  • 150 g cooked broccoli
  • 50 g cooked shelled edamame
  • 20 g reduced-sugar teriyaki sauce
  • 5 g sesame seeds
  • 5 g fresh scallions
  • 5 g fresh ginger

Directions

  1. Weigh 140 g raw Atlantic salmon and cook without added oil until done.
  2. Cook the brown basmati rice and weigh 90 g after cooking.
  3. Cook the broccoli and shelled edamame and weigh after cooking.
  4. Brush the salmon with part of the teriyaki sauce during the final few minutes of cooking.
  5. Place the rice in a bowl and add the broccoli and edamame.
  6. Place the cooked salmon on top and add the remaining teriyaki sauce.
  7. Finish with sesame seeds, fresh ginger and scallions.
