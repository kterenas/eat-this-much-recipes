Greek-Yogurt Chicken Ranch Wrap

A high-protein chicken ranch wrap with an Ole High Fiber tortilla, creamy Greek-yogurt ranch, avocado, cheddar, lettuce and tomato.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken is weighed raw. Use one Ole High Fiber tortilla wrap. No additional cooking oil is used.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 1 Ole High Fiber tortilla wrap
  • 75 g tomato
  • 50 g lettuce
  • 30 g avocado
  • 20 g reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 60 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 3 g dry ranch seasoning
  • 5 g lime juice
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Weigh 170 g raw chicken breast and cook thoroughly without added oil.
  2. Mix the Greek yogurt, ranch seasoning and lime juice to make the ranch sauce.
  3. Warm one Ole High Fiber tortilla until soft and pliable.
  4. Layer the lettuce and tomato in the center of the tortilla.
  5. Slice the cooked chicken and place it over the vegetables.
  6. Add the avocado and reduced-fat cheddar.
  7. Spoon the Greek-yogurt ranch sauce over the chicken.
  8. Fold in the sides, roll tightly and serve.
