Banana-Peanut Butter Protein Pancakes

Soft banana-oat pancakes with peanut butter flavor and a creamy Greek-yogurt topping.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Oats are weighed dry. Banana, egg whites, egg, powdered peanut butter and Greek yogurt are measured in grams.

Ingredients

  • 75 g banana
  • 25 g dry old-fashioned oats
  • 175 g liquid egg whites
  • 50 g whole egg
  • 20 g powdered peanut butter
  • 100 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 g ground cinnamon
  • 2 g vanilla extract
  • 2 g baking powder

Directions

  1. Mash the banana in a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the oats, egg whites, whole egg, powdered peanut butter, cinnamon, vanilla and baking powder.
  3. Blend or whisk until a pancake batter forms.
  4. Cook in a nonstick skillet using cooking spray until golden on both sides and cooked through.
  5. Top with the Greek yogurt and serve.
