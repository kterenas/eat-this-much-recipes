Teriyaki Beef & Pineapple Brown Rice Bowl

Lean ground beef, brown basmati rice, pineapple, broccoli and red bell pepper tossed with a savory teriyaki sauce.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Beef, brown rice and broccoli are weighed cooked. Pineapple is weighed drained.

Ingredients

  • 130 g cooked 95% lean ground beef
  • 100 g cooked brown basmati rice
  • 80 g pineapple chunks, drained
  • 100 g cooked broccoli
  • 50 g red bell pepper
  • 25 g reduced-sodium teriyaki sauce
  • 3 g sesame oil

Directions

  1. Cook the brown basmati rice and weigh 100 g cooked.
  2. Cook the 95% lean ground beef thoroughly and weigh 130 g after cooking.
  3. Cook the broccoli and weigh 100 g cooked.
  4. Add the red bell pepper, pineapple and teriyaki sauce to the cooked beef and heat until warmed through.
  5. Serve the teriyaki beef mixture with the brown rice and broccoli.
  6. Finish with the measured sesame oil.
