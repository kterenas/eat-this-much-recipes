Turkey Sausage, Sweet Potato & Egg Scramble

A hearty high-protein breakfast with lean turkey sausage, fluffy egg whites, roasted sweet potato, vegetables and melted cheddar cheese.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 15 minutes

Measurement rules: Turkey sausage and egg whites are weighed raw. Sweet potato is weighed after cooking. Vegetables are weighed raw. Cheddar cheese is weighed as packaged.

Ingredients

  • 100 g raw lean turkey breakfast sausage
  • 180 g raw liquid egg whites
  • 130 g cooked sweet potato
  • 50 g raw baby spinach
  • 50 g raw red bell pepper
  • 30 g raw onion
  • 20 g reduced-fat shredded cheddar cheese
  • 3 g olive oil
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook sweet potato separately until tender. Dice and weigh 130 g after cooking.
  2. Heat olive oil in a nonstick skillet. Add turkey sausage and crumble while cooking until it reaches 165°F.
  3. Add onion and red bell pepper. Cook for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Add cooked sweet potato, smoked paprika, garlic powder and black pepper. Stir until heated through.
  5. Add spinach and cook until wilted.
  6. Reduce heat to medium-low. Pour in egg whites and scramble gently until fully set.
  7. Sprinkle cheddar over the scramble. Cover briefly until melted and serve.
