Thai Coconut Chicken & Vegetable Soup

A creamy Thai-inspired soup with tender chicken breast, light coconut milk, mushrooms, red peppers, spinach, ginger, lime and brown basmati rice.

Servings: 1

Measurement rules: Chicken is weighed raw. Brown basmati rice is weighed after cooking. Vegetables, ginger and garlic are weighed raw. Coconut milk is measured as packaged.

Ingredients

  • 180 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 120 g canned light coconut milk
  • 90 g cooked Wegmans Basmati Brown Rice
  • 100 g raw mushrooms
  • 75 g raw red bell pepper
  • 60 g raw baby spinach
  • 40 g raw onion
  • 10 g fresh ginger
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 250 g low-sodium chicken broth
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 10 g low-sodium soy sauce
  • 15 g fresh lime juice
  • 10 g red curry paste
  • 5 g fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. Cook brown basmati rice separately. Weigh 90 g after cooking.
  2. Cut raw chicken breast into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Heat olive oil in a soup pot. Add onion, red bell pepper and mushrooms. Cook for 4–5 minutes.
  4. Add garlic, ginger and red curry paste. Stir for 1 minute.
  5. Add chicken broth, light coconut milk and soy sauce.
  6. Add chicken and simmer gently for 10–15 minutes, until chicken reaches 165°F.
  7. Add spinach and cook until wilted.
  8. Stir in cooked brown rice and heat through.
  9. Remove from heat. Stir in lime juice, garnish with cilantro and serve.
