Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken Banza

A creamy garlic Parmesan pasta made with Banza chickpea pasta, rotisserie chicken breast, broccoli and a high-protein cottage cheese sauce.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Banza pasta, chicken and broccoli are weighed cooked.

Ingredients

  • 60 g cooked Banza chickpea pasta
  • 120 g cooked rotisserie chicken breast, skin removed
  • 100 g cooked broccoli
  • 60 g low-fat cottage cheese
  • 12 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 3 g olive oil
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 1 g Italian seasoning
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the Banza chickpea pasta and weigh 60 g cooked.
  2. Cook the broccoli until tender-crisp and weigh 100 g cooked.
  3. Blend the cottage cheese, garlic and a small amount of warm pasta water until smooth and creamy.
  4. Warm the rotisserie chicken breast and weigh 120 g cooked.
  5. Combine the Banza pasta, chicken, broccoli and cottage cheese sauce.
  6. Stir in the Parmesan, olive oil, Italian seasoning and black pepper. Add a little pasta water if needed to reach your preferred consistency.
