Chicken Parmesan Bowl

A lighter chicken Parmesan bowl with Banza chickpea pasta, marinara, broccoli, melted mozzarella and Parmesan.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken is weighed raw. Banza is weighed dry before cooking. Broccoli is weighed after cooking. No additional cooking oil is used.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 56 g dry Banza chickpea pasta
  • 125 g marinara sauce
  • 125 g cooked broccoli
  • 25 g part-skim mozzarella cheese
  • 10 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 g garlic powder
  • 1 g Italian seasoning
  • 1 g crushed red pepper flakes
  • 5 g fresh basil

Directions

  1. Weigh 170 g raw chicken breast. Season with garlic powder and Italian seasoning and cook without added oil.
  2. Cook 56 g dry Banza chickpea pasta according to package directions and drain.
  3. Cook the broccoli and weigh 125 g cooked.
  4. Warm the marinara sauce.
  5. Combine the Banza, broccoli and marinara in a bowl.
  6. Slice the chicken and place it over the pasta.
  7. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan and heat until the mozzarella melts.
  8. Finish with crushed red pepper flakes and fresh basil.
