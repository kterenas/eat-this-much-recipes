Blackened Tilapia, Brown Rice & Avocado Bowl

A high-protein Cajun-style tilapia bowl with cooked brown basmati rice, fresh corn and pepper salsa, avocado and creamy lime-yogurt sauce.

Servings: 1

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 15 minutes

Measurement rules: Tilapia, avocado, bell pepper, red onion and cilantro are weighed raw. Brown rice and corn are weighed after cooking. Greek yogurt is weighed as packaged. All quantities are for one serving.

Ingredients

  • 220 g raw boneless tilapia fillet
  • 100 g cooked Wegmans Basmati Brown Rice
  • 50 g raw avocado flesh
  • 40 g cooked corn kernels
  • 60 g raw red bell pepper
  • 30 g raw red onion
  • 60 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 15 g fresh lime juice
  • 5 g fresh cilantro, raw
  • 2 g smoked paprika
  • 2 g chili powder
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g ground cumin
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Pat dry the raw tilapia and season with smoked paprika, chili powder, garlic powder, cumin and black pepper.
  2. Heat 5 g olive oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Cook tilapia approximately 3–5 minutes per side until the internal temperature reaches 145°F.
  3. Warm 100 g previously cooked brown basmati rice.
  4. Combine 40 g cooked corn, 60 g diced bell pepper, 30 g diced red onion, 5 g chopped cilantro and 10 g lime juice.
  5. Mix 60 g nonfat Greek yogurt with the remaining 5 g lime juice.
  6. Place rice in a bowl. Add the blackened tilapia, corn salsa and 50 g sliced avocado. Drizzle with lime-yogurt sauce.
