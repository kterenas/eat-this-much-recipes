Creamy Buffalo Chicken Mac & Cheese Bowl

Buffalo chicken, Banza chickpea pasta and broccoli tossed in a creamy cottage-cheese and cheddar sauce.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken, pasta and broccoli are weighed cooked.

Ingredients

  • 140 g cooked rotisserie chicken breast, skin removed
  • 85 g cooked Banza chickpea pasta
  • 100 g cooked broccoli
  • 30 g low-fat cottage cheese
  • 15 g reduced-fat shredded cheddar cheese
  • 20 g buffalo sauce
  • 3 g olive oil

Directions

  1. Cook the Banza chickpea pasta according to package directions and weigh 85 g cooked.
  2. Cook the broccoli and weigh 100 g cooked.
  3. Blend or stir the cottage cheese with the buffalo sauce until creamy.
  4. Heat the chicken, pasta and broccoli with the olive oil in a skillet.
  5. Stir in the buffalo cottage-cheese sauce and cheddar. Heat just until the cheese melts and everything is creamy.
