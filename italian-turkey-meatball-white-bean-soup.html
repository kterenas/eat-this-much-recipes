Italian Turkey Meatball & White Bean Soup

Tender turkey meatballs, white beans, spinach and tomatoes in an Italian-style broth with Parmesan cheese.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Ground turkey is weighed raw. Cannellini beans are weighed after draining. Vegetables are weighed raw.

Ingredients

  • 160 g raw 93% lean ground turkey
  • 90 g canned cannellini beans, drained
  • 120 g canned diced tomatoes
  • 75 g raw baby spinach
  • 50 g raw carrots
  • 50 g raw onion
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 300 g low-sodium chicken broth
  • 15 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 2 g Italian seasoning
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 5 g fresh parsley

Directions

  1. Mix ground turkey with garlic powder, half the Italian seasoning, black pepper and half the Parmesan.
  2. Form into small meatballs.
  3. Heat olive oil in a soup pot and brown the meatballs.
  4. Add onion and carrots. Cook for 4–5 minutes.
  5. Add garlic and remaining Italian seasoning.
  6. Add chicken broth, tomatoes and drained white beans.
  7. Simmer for 12–15 minutes until meatballs reach 165°F.
  8. Add spinach and cook until wilted.
  9. Serve topped with remaining Parmesan and parsley.
