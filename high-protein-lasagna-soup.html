High-Protein Lasagna Soup

A hearty Italian-inspired soup featuring lean ground beef, Banza chickpea pasta, rich tomato broth, ricotta and Parmesan.

Servings: 1

Measurement rules: Ground beef is weighed raw. Banza pasta is weighed dry. Vegetables are weighed raw. Cheese and canned ingredients are measured as packaged.

Ingredients

  • 150 g raw 95% lean ground beef
  • 40 g dry Banza chickpea pasta
  • 150 g canned crushed tomatoes
  • 40 g part-skim ricotta cheese
  • 10 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 50 g raw baby spinach
  • 40 g raw onion
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 250 g low-sodium beef broth
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 15 g tomato paste
  • 2 g Italian seasoning
  • 1 g dried basil
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a soup pot over medium heat. Add ground beef and onion. Cook until browned.
  2. Add garlic, tomato paste, Italian seasoning, dried basil and black pepper. Stir for 1 minute.
  3. Add crushed tomatoes and beef broth. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add 40 g dry Banza chickpea pasta directly to the soup. Cook according to package directions, stirring occasionally. Add water if needed.
  5. Add baby spinach and cook until wilted.
  6. Remove from heat. Top with ricotta cheese and grated Parmesan.
  7. Serve warm.
