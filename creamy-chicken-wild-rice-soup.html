Creamy Chicken & Wild Rice Soup

A hearty, high-protein dinner soup with tender chicken, wild rice, carrots, celery and a creamy Greek yogurt broth.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken is weighed raw. Wild rice is weighed cooked. Vegetables are weighed raw. Olive oil is measured separately.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw skinless chicken breast
  • 100 g cooked wild rice
  • 60 g raw carrots
  • 60 g raw celery
  • 50 g raw onion
  • 5 g fresh garlic
  • 300 g low-sodium chicken broth
  • 100 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 10 g olive oil
  • 1 g dried thyme
  • 1 g dried rosemary
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook wild rice and measure 100 g cooked.
  2. Cook 170 g raw chicken breast to 165°F, then shred or dice.
  3. Heat olive oil in a soup pot over medium heat.
  4. Add carrots, celery and onion. Sauté until softened.
  5. Add garlic, thyme, rosemary and black pepper.
  6. Pour in the chicken broth and bring to a gentle simmer.
  7. Add cooked chicken and wild rice. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  8. Remove from heat.
  9. Temper Greek yogurt with a little warm broth.
  10. Stir yogurt into soup without boiling.
  11. Serve warm.
