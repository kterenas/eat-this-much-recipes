Cheesy Ground Chicken, Broccoli & Brown Rice Casserole

Creamy, cheesy, high-protein family casserole with lean ground chicken, broccoli and brown rice.

Servings: 4

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Measurement rules: Ground chicken, broccoli, onion and garlic are weighed raw. Brown rice is weighed after cooking. All quantities listed are for the entire four-serving casserole, not one serving.

Ingredients

  • 800 g raw 98% lean ground chicken
  • 400 g cooked Wegmans Basmati Brown Rice
  • 500 g raw broccoli florets
  • 120 g reduced-fat sharp cheddar cheese
  • 240 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 60 g reduced-fat cream cheese
  • 120 g reduced-sodium chicken broth
  • 100 g raw yellow onion
  • 12 g raw garlic
  • 12 g olive oil
  • 15 g Dijon mustard
  • 4 g smoked paprika
  • 2 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Cook rice and weigh 400 g after cooking. Steam broccoli for 3–4 minutes.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet. Cook diced onion and minced garlic, then add ground chicken. Cook until the chicken reaches 165°F.
  3. Reduce heat. Stir in broth, cream cheese, Dijon mustard, smoked paprika and black pepper.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in Greek yogurt.
  5. Fold in cooked rice, broccoli and 60 g cheddar.
  6. Transfer to a 9 × 13-inch baking dish. Top with remaining 60 g cheddar.
  7. Bake uncovered for 15–20 minutes until heated through and cheese melts.
  8. Rest 5 minutes. Weigh the finished casserole and divide into four equal portions.
