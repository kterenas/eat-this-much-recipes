High-Protein Baked Ziti with Lean Ground Beef & Banza Pasta

A hearty, high-protein baked pasta casserole made with lean ground beef, Banza chickpea pasta, marinara sauce, cottage cheese and melted mozzarella.

Servings: 4

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Measurement rules: Ground beef, spinach, onion, garlic and basil are weighed raw. Banza pasta is weighed dry, before cooking. Cottage cheese, marinara and shredded cheeses are weighed as packaged. All quantities are for the entire four-serving casserole, not one serving.

Ingredients

  • 680 g raw 96% lean ground beef
  • 200 g dry Banza chickpea penne pasta
  • 400 g no-added-sugar marinara sauce
  • 240 g low-fat 2% cottage cheese
  • 100 g part-skim shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 20 g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 120 g raw baby spinach
  • 100 g raw yellow onion
  • 12 g raw garlic
  • 8 g olive oil
  • 5 g dried Italian seasoning
  • 2 g black pepper
  • 10 g fresh basil, raw

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Cook 200 g dry Banza chickpea penne in boiling water until approximately 2 minutes shy of package directions. Drain well.
  2. Heat 8 g olive oil in a large skillet. Add diced onion and minced garlic. Cook briefly, then add ground beef. Cook until browned and the beef reaches 160°F.
  3. Stir in 400 g marinara sauce, Italian seasoning, black pepper and baby spinach. Simmer until the spinach wilts.
  4. Remove skillet from heat. Fold in the cooked Banza pasta and 240 g cottage cheese.
  5. Transfer the mixture to a 9 × 13-inch baking dish. Top evenly with 100 g part-skim mozzarella and 20 g Parmesan cheese.
  6. Bake uncovered for 15–20 minutes until heated through and the cheese is melted.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes. Garnish with 10 g fresh basil. Weigh the finished casserole and divide into four equal portions.
