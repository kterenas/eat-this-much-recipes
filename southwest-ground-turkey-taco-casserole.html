Southwest Ground Turkey Taco Casserole

A flavorful high-protein casserole with lean ground turkey, black beans, sweet corn, peppers, salsa and melted reduced-fat cheese.

Servings: 4

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Measurement rules: Ground turkey, bell pepper, onion, garlic and cilantro are weighed raw. Black beans are weighed after draining and rinsing. Corn is weighed cooked. All quantities are for the entire four-serving recipe. Nutrition should be calculated per serving.

Ingredients

  • 800 g raw 93% lean ground turkey
  • 240 g canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 160 g cooked sweet corn
  • 160 g raw red bell pepper
  • 100 g raw yellow onion
  • 240 g chunky tomato salsa
  • 100 g reduced-fat Mexican cheese blend
  • 160 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 12 g raw garlic
  • 8 g chili powder
  • 5 g ground cumin
  • 4 g smoked paprika
  • 2 g dried oregano
  • 12 g fresh cilantro, raw
  • 20 g fresh lime juice

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Prepare a 9-by-13-inch baking dish.
  2. Brown ground turkey in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat, breaking it into small pieces.
  3. Add diced yellow onion, red bell pepper and minced garlic. Cook until vegetables soften and turkey reaches 165°F.
  4. Stir in chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, drained black beans, cooked corn and chunky salsa. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Transfer mixture to the baking dish and spread evenly. Top with reduced-fat Mexican cheese blend.
  6. Bake uncovered for 15–20 minutes until heated through and the cheese is melted.
  7. Allow to rest for 5 minutes. Weigh the finished casserole and divide into four equal portions.
  8. Top each serving with 40 g nonfat Greek yogurt, 3 g cilantro and 5 g lime juice.
