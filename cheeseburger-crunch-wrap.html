Cheeseburger Crunch Wrap

A crispy, cheesy, high-protein cheeseburger wrap with lean ground beef, fresh vegetables and a creamy Greek yogurt burger sauce.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 10 minutes

Measurement rules: Ground beef is weighed raw before cooking. Vegetables are weighed raw, except pickles, which are weighed drained. Tortilla, cheese, yogurt, ketchup and mustard are weighed as packaged. Olive oil is measured separately.

Ingredients

  • 170 g raw 95% lean ground beef
  • 45 g Ole High Fiber tortilla (1 tortilla)
  • 30 g reduced-fat shredded cheddar cheese
  • 40 g raw romaine lettuce
  • 60 g raw tomato
  • 30 g drained dill pickles
  • 25 g raw red onion
  • 45 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 15 g ketchup
  • 10 g yellow mustard
  • 3 g olive oil
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a skillet. Add raw ground beef, garlic powder, smoked paprika and black pepper. Cook thoroughly to an internal temperature of 160°F.
  2. Mix Greek yogurt, ketchup and yellow mustard to prepare the burger sauce.
  3. Warm the tortilla briefly until flexible.
  4. Place cooked beef and shredded cheddar in the center of the tortilla.
  5. Add lettuce, tomato, pickles, onion and burger sauce.
  6. Fold tightly into a wrap, tucking in the ends. Serve any excess vegetables or sauce on the side.
  7. Toast seam-side down in a nonstick skillet for 2–3 minutes per side until crisp and golden.
