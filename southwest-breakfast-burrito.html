Southwest Breakfast Burrito

A high-protein breakfast burrito with seasoned turkey, eggs, avocado, cheddar, vegetables and salsa.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Ground turkey is weighed raw. Use one Ole High Fiber tortilla.

Ingredients

  • 1 Ole High Fiber tortilla wrap
  • 75 g raw 93% lean ground turkey
  • 100 g liquid egg whites
  • 50 g whole egg
  • 25 g reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 40 g avocado
  • 50 g salsa
  • 40 g bell pepper
  • 25 g onion
  • 2 g ground cumin
  • 1 g chili powder

Directions

  1. Cook the ground turkey with cumin and chili powder.
  2. Add the bell pepper and onion and cook until softened.
  3. Add the whole egg and egg whites and scramble until cooked.
  4. Warm the Ole High Fiber tortilla.
  5. Add the turkey and egg mixture to the tortilla.
  6. Top with cheddar, avocado and salsa.
  7. Fold in the sides, roll tightly and serve.
