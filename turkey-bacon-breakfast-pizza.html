Turkey Bacon Breakfast Pizza

A crispy, cheesy high-protein breakfast pizza with turkey bacon, egg whites, mozzarella, fresh vegetables and salsa on a high-fiber tortilla.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Measurement rules: Turkey bacon and egg whites are weighed raw. Vegetables are weighed raw. Cheese and salsa are weighed as packaged. Use the tortilla's actual package weight.

Ingredients

  • 1 Ole High Fiber tortilla (approximately 45 g)
  • 75 g raw turkey bacon
  • 200 g raw liquid egg whites
  • 45 g part-skim shredded mozzarella
  • 50 g raw red bell pepper
  • 40 g raw baby spinach
  • 25 g raw red onion
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 40 g salsa
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (205°C). Place tortilla on a baking sheet.
  2. Cook turkey bacon according to package instructions until fully cooked. Chop.
  3. Heat olive oil in a skillet. Add egg whites, garlic powder and black pepper. Scramble until just set.
  4. Spread salsa evenly over tortilla.
  5. Add scrambled egg whites, turkey bacon, bell pepper, red onion and spinach.
  6. Sprinkle mozzarella over toppings.
  7. Bake for 7–10 minutes until cheese melts and tortilla edges are crisp.
