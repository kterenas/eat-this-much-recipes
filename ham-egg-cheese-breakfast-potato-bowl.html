Ham, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Potato Bowl

A savory high-protein breakfast bowl with ham, eggs, potatoes, vegetables, cheddar and avocado.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Potatoes are weighed cooked. Ham is weighed ready-to-eat. Egg whites and whole egg are weighed before cooking.

Ingredients

  • 100 g lean ham, diced
  • 175 g liquid egg whites
  • 50 g whole egg
  • 130 g cooked baby potatoes
  • 75 g bell pepper
  • 50 g onion
  • 20 g reduced-fat cheddar cheese
  • 40 g avocado
  • 1 g garlic powder
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g black pepper
  • Cooking spray

Directions

  1. Cook the baby potatoes and weigh 130 g cooked.
  2. Coat a skillet with cooking spray.
  3. Cook the bell pepper and onion until softened.
  4. Add the ham and potatoes and cook until heated and lightly browned.
  5. Add the egg whites and whole egg and cook until set.
  6. Season with garlic powder, smoked paprika and black pepper.
  7. Transfer to a bowl and top with cheddar cheese and avocado.
