Philly Cheesesteak Potato Bowl

Lean beef, potatoes, peppers, onions and mushrooms topped with melted provolone for a cheesesteak-inspired high-protein lunch.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Ground beef is weighed raw. Potatoes are weighed after cooking.

Ingredients

  • 150 g raw 95% lean ground beef
  • 130 g cooked baby potatoes
  • 100 g bell pepper
  • 75 g onion
  • 100 g mushrooms
  • 25 g provolone cheese
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 5 g Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 g fresh garlic
  • 1 g black pepper

Directions

  1. Cook the baby potatoes and weigh 130 g cooked.
  2. Heat the olive oil and sauté the pepper, onion and mushrooms until tender.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook thoroughly.
  4. Add the garlic, Worcestershire sauce and black pepper.
  5. Add the potatoes and toss everything together.
  6. Transfer to a bowl, top with provolone and heat until melted.
