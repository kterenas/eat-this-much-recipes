Thai Coconut Curry Tilapia & Quinoa Bowl

Tender tilapia simmered in a creamy Thai-inspired red coconut curry with broccoli and red bell pepper, served over cooked white quinoa.

Servings: 1

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Measurement rules: Tilapia, broccoli, bell pepper, ginger, garlic and cilantro are weighed raw. Quinoa is weighed after cooking. Coconut milk, curry paste and soy sauce are weighed as packaged. All quantities are for one serving.

Ingredients

  • 220 g raw boneless tilapia fillet
  • 90 g cooked Wegmans White Quinoa
  • 100 g canned light coconut milk
  • 100 g raw broccoli florets
  • 70 g raw red bell pepper
  • 15 g Thai red curry paste
  • 5 g olive oil
  • 15 g fresh lime juice
  • 5 g fresh ginger, raw
  • 5 g garlic, raw
  • 5 g fresh cilantro, raw
  • 10 g reduced-sodium soy sauce

Directions

  1. Prepare the Wegmans White Quinoa according to package directions. Weigh 90 g after cooking.
  2. Heat 5 g olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add minced ginger and garlic. Cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Stir in 15 g Thai red curry paste, followed by 100 g light coconut milk and 10 g reduced-sodium soy sauce. Stir until combined.
  4. Add broccoli florets and sliced red bell pepper. Simmer for approximately 4–5 minutes.
  5. Cut 220 g raw tilapia into large pieces. Nestle into the curry sauce, cover and gently simmer for 5–8 minutes, or until the fish reaches an internal temperature of 145°F.
  6. Stir in 15 g fresh lime juice. Serve the curry over 90 g cooked quinoa and garnish with 5 g fresh cilantro.
