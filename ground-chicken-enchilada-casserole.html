Cheesy Ground Chicken Enchilada Casserole

A hearty, high-protein family casserole with seasoned ground chicken, high-fiber tortillas, black beans, red enchilada sauce and melted cheese.

Servings: 4

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Measurement rules: Ground chicken, bell pepper, onion, garlic and cilantro are weighed raw. Black beans are weighed after draining and rinsing. Tortillas, cheese, enchilada sauce and yogurt are weighed as packaged. All quantities are for the entire four-serving casserole, not one serving.

Ingredients

  • 800 g raw 98% lean ground chicken
  • 180 g Ole High Fiber tortillas (4 whole wraps)
  • 200 g canned black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 320 g red enchilada sauce
  • 120 g reduced-fat Mexican cheese blend
  • 160 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 160 g raw red bell pepper
  • 100 g raw yellow onion
  • 12 g raw garlic
  • 8 g olive oil
  • 8 g chili powder
  • 5 g ground cumin
  • 4 g smoked paprika
  • 12 g fresh cilantro, raw
  • 20 g fresh lime juice

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Prepare a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.
  2. Heat 8 g olive oil in a large skillet. Add diced yellow onion, red bell pepper and minced garlic. Cook for about 3 minutes.
  3. Add ground chicken and cook until browned and the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
  4. Stir in chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, drained black beans and 160 g enchilada sauce. Simmer for 3 minutes.
  5. Spread 80 g enchilada sauce across the bottom of the baking dish. Layer 2 whole tortillas, half the chicken mixture and 60 g cheese.
  6. Repeat with the remaining 2 tortillas, chicken mixture and 60 g cheese. Pour the remaining 80 g enchilada sauce evenly over the top.
  7. Bake uncovered for 15–20 minutes until heated through and the cheese has melted.
  8. Rest for 5 minutes. Weigh the finished casserole and divide into four equal portions.
  9. Top each serving with 40 g nonfat Greek yogurt, 3 g cilantro and 5 g lime juice.
