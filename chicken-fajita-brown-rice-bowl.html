Chicken Fajita Brown Rice Bowl

A high-protein chicken fajita bowl with brown basmati rice, black beans, vegetables, avocado and Greek yogurt.

Servings: 1

Measurement rule: Chicken, rice, bell pepper and onion are measured by cooked weight. Black beans are weighed after draining.

Ingredients

  • 165 g cooked skinless chicken breast
  • 40 g cooked brown basmati rice
  • 100 g cooked bell pepper
  • 75 g cooked onion
  • 25 g canned black beans, drained
  • 50 g salsa
  • 70 g avocado
  • 50 g nonfat plain Greek yogurt
  • 10 g lime juice
  • 2 g ground cumin
  • 2 g chili powder
  • 1 g smoked paprika
  • 5 g fresh cilantro

Directions

  1. Cook the chicken thoroughly and weigh 165 g after cooking.
  2. Cook the brown basmati rice and weigh 40 g after cooking.
  3. Cook the bell pepper and onion in a nonstick skillet until tender.
  4. Season the chicken with cumin, chili powder and smoked paprika.
  5. Place the cooked brown rice in a bowl and add the chicken, black beans, bell pepper and onion.
  6. Top with salsa, avocado, Greek yogurt, lime juice and fresh cilantro.
